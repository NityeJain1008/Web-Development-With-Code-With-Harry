AI Self Driving Cars

Self-driving cars are vehicles that use artificial intelligence, sensors, cameras, radar, and LiDAR to navigate roads without requiring constant human control. They can detect obstacles, follow traffic rules, and make driving decisions in real time. This technology aims to improve road safety, reduce traffic congestion, and provide greater mobility for people who cannot drive. Although autonomous vehicles continue to advance rapidly, challenges such as complex road conditions, regulations, and public trust must still be addressed before they become widely adopted.

AI Resume Analyser

The AI Resume Analyzer is a web-based application that uses artificial intelligence to evaluate resumes and provide personalized feedback. Users can upload their resumes to receive insights on ATS compatibility, keyword optimization, formatting, and skill gaps based on job descriptions. The platform helps job seekers improve their resumes by offering actionable suggestions, increasing their chances of passing applicant tracking systems and securing interview opportunities.
